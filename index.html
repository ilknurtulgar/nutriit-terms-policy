Frequently asked questions

How is the score calculated?

Nutriit uses fixed rules based on energy, sugars, saturated fat and salt (which lower the score) and fiber, protein and fruit, vegetable and legume content (which raise it). Rules differ for beverages, cheese, and fats, oils and nuts.

Is this medical advice?

No. Nutriit gives general information only and does not say whether a product is healthy or safe for you. Always check the packaging, especially for allergens.

What if data is missing?

Nutriit shows “insufficient data” instead of guessing a score, and lists what is missing.

What if a product is not found?

Nutriit tells you clearly that the product is not in the database. It never invents product data.

Does it cost anything?

Nutriit is free to use.

Which languages are supported?

English and Turkish.
