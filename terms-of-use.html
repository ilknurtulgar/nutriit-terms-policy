02 Description of Service

Nutriit lets you scan the barcode of a packaged food product and see product information from Open Food Facts: a 0–10 nutrition score with the reasons behind it, nutrition facts, ingredients, allergens and comparable products in the same category. Your recent scans are kept on your device. The App is free to use and does not require an account.

  • The nutrition score is calculated by fixed, versioned rules from the nutrition values available for the product. It does not state that a product is healthy, unhealthy or safe for you.
  • Do not rely on the App to manage allergies, intolerances or medical conditions.

04 Product Data May Be Incomplete or Inaccurate

  • Product data comes from Open Food Facts, a collaborative database maintained by volunteers. It may be missing, outdated or incorrect.
  • If allergen information is missing, this does not mean the product is free of allergens. Always check the product packaging.
  • When the values needed for a score are missing, the App shows "insufficient data" instead of a score.
  • Comparable products are suggested only from products with enough data in the same category and the same comparison basis. A suggestion is not a recommendation for your personal diet.
