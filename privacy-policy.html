Nutriit Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

01 Introduction

Nutriit ("we", "our", "us"), developed by İlknur Tulgar, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Nutriit mobile app ("the App") handles when you scan food products, and why.

In short: you do not need an account, camera images never leave your phone, your recent scans stay on your device, and anonymous usage and crash reports are sent only if you allow it.

02 Information We Handle

  • Camera — When you start a scan, the App asks for camera access. The camera image is processed on your device only to detect a barcode. We never store, upload or share camera images.
  • Barcode and product lookups — When a barcode is detected, the barcode number is sent to the Open Food Facts database to retrieve product information. To find comparable products, the product's category is also sent to Open Food Facts. No other information about you is included in these requests.
  • Recent scans — Your last five scanned products are saved only on your device so you can reopen them. They are not sent to us and are removed when you uninstall the App.
  • Your consent choice — Whether you allowed or declined usage and crash reports is saved only on your device.
  • Usage data (optional) — Only if you allow it, we use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used. We record these events: app opened, scan started and completed, product found or not found, product detail viewed, score available or unavailable, comparable products viewed or selected, and scan history opened. Events do not contain barcodes, product names, camera images or anything you type. Google Analytics may derive your approximate location (such as country or city) from your IP address; we do not collect precise location.
  • Diagnostic data (optional) — Only if you allow it, Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports with technical details of the error, the App version, your device model, operating system version and a log of recent App events, so that we can fix problems.
  • Device identifiers — If you allow usage and crash reports, Firebase assigns a random app-installation identifier to group them. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA / Advertising ID), we do not show ads and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Support emails — If you contact us using "Contact support", we receive your email address and the content of your message.

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or health data, and we do not create user accounts or profiles.

03 How We Use Information

Information is used only to:

  • Look up the product you scanned and show its nutrition score, ingredients and allergens
  • Find comparable products in the same category
  • Show your recent scans on your device
  • Understand which features are used and where users have difficulties, if you allowed usage reports
  • Diagnose crashes and improve stability, if you allowed crash reports
  • Answer your support requests

We do not sell or rent your information and we do not build advertising profiles.

04 Third-Party Services

  • Open Food Facts — A collaborative, non-profit food products database that provides the product data shown in the App. Like any website you visit, Open Food Facts receives your device's IP address when the App sends a request. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy: https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy
  • Google Firebase — Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics are operated by Google LLC and are used only if you allow them. Data may be processed on servers outside your country; Google applies appropriate safeguards for international transfers, including Standard Contractual Clauses where required. We do not enable Google Analytics advertising features such as Google signals and we do not link analytics data to advertising accounts. See https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • App stores — If you choose "Rate the app", the rating is handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own policies.

05 Your Choice About Usage and Crash Reports

The first time you open the App, we ask whether you want to share anonymous usage statistics and crash reports. Nothing is sent until you allow it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy. When you turn it off, collection stops immediately.

06 Data Storage and Retention

  • Recent scans and your consent choice stay on your device until you uninstall the App.
  • Crash and diagnostic data collected by Crashlytics is kept for up to 90 days.
  • Usage data collected by Google Analytics is kept for the period configured in Google Analytics, which is at most 14 months; aggregated reports may be kept longer.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.

07 Data Security

All requests from the App are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Because the App does not create accounts or store your scans on our servers, there is no personal profile of you held by us. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

08 Children's Privacy

Nutriit is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in jurisdictions such as the EU/EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

09 Your Rights (GDPR / KVKK)

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Data portability, where technically applicable
  • Withdraw consent at any time, which you can also do directly in Settings

Users in Turkey are additionally protected under the Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK No. 6698). To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11. We will respond within 30 days in accordance with applicable law. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

10 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated through the App or on our website, and the "Last updated" date above will change. Continued use of the App after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
